\chapter{Conclusion}\label{chapter:conclusion}

Through a concept-centric literature review and thematic analysis, this thesis identified ten challenges across five themes and 34 solution mechanisms in the NAS-in-FL literature. Table~\ref{table:challenges-and-solutions-overview} records the relationships that source publications report between these mechanisms and challenges. The analysis also retains the NAS method types and FL system characteristics of the reported evidence. These mappings do not establish the applicability of either a challenge or a solution mechanism to a target configuration.

Separating solution mechanisms from the complete NAS-in-FL methods in which they appear makes recurring design components visible across different terminology and implementations. This synthesis provides a structured index for comparing how publications respond to NAS-in-FL challenges and for locating the evidence behind each relationship. It is not a decision rule for selecting mechanisms for a new configuration, and comparative evaluations are required to rank candidate mechanisms.

The central contribution is the concept-centric organization of reported challenge--solution relationships. The prerequisites, costs, and interactions recorded alongside those relationships describe the source evidence rather than support a claim about transfer to other configurations. The reviewed evaluations cover uneven configurations and often describe their FL systems incompletely, which prevents the synthesis from establishing applicability or comparative effectiveness.

Future research should evaluate individual mechanisms and selected combinations under the same NAS design choices and FL system characteristics. These evaluations should represent operational conditions such as scale and participation and should report end-to-end costs alongside architecture quality. Such evidence is required before NAS-in-FL research can establish whether a challenge occurs or a solution mechanism is effective under specified conditions.
