\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our thematic analysis identified ten recurring challenges, organized as subthemes within five themes: \textit{client resource constraints}, \textit{client heterogeneity}, \textit{decentralized search state}, \textit{participation dynamics}, and \textit{search security}. For each challenge, we explain how the identified solution mechanisms address them. See Table~\ref{table:challenges-and-solutions-overview} for an overview.

A challenge may have several solutions, and one solution may address several challenges. For clarity, each solution appears under the challenge it primarily addresses. Links to other challenges are identified as cross-cutting. We report the NAS method types of the source publications to preserve the context of the evidence.
