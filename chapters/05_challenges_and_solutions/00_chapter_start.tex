\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our thematic analysis revealed ten key challenges acting as sub-themes of five themes relating to FL system characteristics: \textit{client resource constraints}, \textit{client heterogeneity}, \textit{decentralized search state}, \textit{participation dynamics}, and \textit{security}.

A challenge may have several solutions, and one solution may address several challenges. For clarity, each solution appears under the challenge it primarily addresses. Links to other challenges are identified as cross-cutting. We generally qualify challenges and solutions with the NAS method types of the publications they orignate from.
