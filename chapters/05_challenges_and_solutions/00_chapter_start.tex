\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our analysis revealed ten key challenges related to five FL system characteristic themes: \textit{client resource constraints}, \textit{client heterogeneity}, \textit{decentralized search state}, \textit{participation dynamics}, and \textit{security}.

The relationship between challenges and solutions is many-to-many because a challenge may be addressed by several solutions, while a solution may address multiple challenges. To provide a clear structure, each solution is presented under the challenge it primarily addresses. When a solution also addresses other challenges, these secondary relationships are identified as cross-cutting.
