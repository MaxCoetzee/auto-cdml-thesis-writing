\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our analysis revealed ten key challenges related to five FL system characteristic themes: \textit{client resource constraints}, \textit{client heterogeneity}, \textit{decentralized search state}, \textit{participation dynamics}, and \textit{security}.

A challenge may have several solutions, and one solution may address several challenges. For clarity, each solution appears under the challenge it primarily addresses. Links to other challenges are identified as cross-cutting.

We generally qualify challenges and solutions with the NAS method types of the NAS-in-FL methods that represent them orignate from.
