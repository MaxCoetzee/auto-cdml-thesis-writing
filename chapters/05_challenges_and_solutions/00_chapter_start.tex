\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our thematic analysis revealed five overarching themes for challenges: \textit{Resource Burden of NAS in FL}, \textit{Client Heterogeneity}, \textit{Federated Search-State Coordination}, \textit{Disrupted Architecture Search} and \textit{Architecture Search Security}. Each solution remains with the primary challenge that it addresses, while the subsections identify recurring patterns within the broader themes.

If challenges or solutions only are only reported for certain FL system characteristics or NAS search strategies/search spaces, then we qualify them accordingly.
