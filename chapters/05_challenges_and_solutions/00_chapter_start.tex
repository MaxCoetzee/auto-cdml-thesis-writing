\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our thematic analysis revealed five overarching themes for challenges: \textit{Resource Burdens of NAS in FL}, \textit{Client Heterogeneity}, \textit{Federated Search-State Coordination}, \textit{Disrupted Architecture Search} and \textit{Architecture Search Security}. Each challenge expresses one central analytical claim, although several different solution mechanisms may address it. Each solution remains with the primary challenge that it addresses, while the subsections identify recurring patterns within the broader themes.

If challenges or solutions are reported only for certain FL system characteristics or NAS search strategies or search spaces, then we qualify them accordingly.

- FL system characteristics for which the challenges are applicable/more severe
- NAS types based on NAS in FL and NAS 1000 papers categorisation
