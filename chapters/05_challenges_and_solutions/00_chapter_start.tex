\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our analysis revealed five overarching themes of related challenges: \textit{Resource Burdens of NAS in FL}, \textit{Client Heterogeneity}, \textit{Federated Search-State Coordination}, \textit{Disrupted Architecture Search} and \textit{Architecture Search Security}. 

The applicability and severity of a challenge depends on the NAS-in-FL scenario. For each challenge, we report the least-specifc NAS-in-FL scenario for which the challenge is made relevant in the reviewed literature.

We qualify challenges by the least specific combination of FL system characeristics and NAS method configuration to capture the most general scenario for which they are relevant. Solutions to challenges can operate at the same level of scenario specificity or apply to more specific scenarios.
