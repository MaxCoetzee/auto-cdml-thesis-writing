\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

Our analysis revealed ten challenges related to five FL system characteristics from which they arise: client resource constraints, client heterogeneity, decentralized search state, participation dynamics, and security. The challenges describe consequences that become specific to, or are intensified by, performing architecture search through FL.

The applicability and severity of each challenge depend on the NAS-in-FL scenario. We therefore state the FL conditions and NAS configuration that make a challenge relevant.

The relationship between challenges and solutions is many-to-many. A challenge may admit several responses, and one response may address more than one challenge. Each solution is described under the challenge that best captures its primary purpose. Cross-references identify additional directly supported relationships.
