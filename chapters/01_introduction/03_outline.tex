This thesis is structured as follows. Chapter~\ref{chapter:background} introduces NAS, FL, and their combination in NAS-in-FL. Chapter~\ref{chapter:method} describes the literature selection, thematic analysis, and relationship mapping. Chapter~\ref{chapter:related-work} reviews prior NAS-in-FL syntheses and positions this review. Chapter~\ref{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol} presents the ten identified challenges and 34 solution mechanisms across five themes. Chapter~\ref{chapter:discussion} interprets the findings and discusses their implications, limitations, and directions for future research. Finally, Chapter~\ref{chapter:conclusion} summarizes the thesis and its main contribution.
