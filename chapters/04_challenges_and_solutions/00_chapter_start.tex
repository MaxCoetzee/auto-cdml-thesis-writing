\chapter{Challenges and Solutions for Using NAS in FL}\label{chapter:nas_in_fl_ch_and_sol}

We identified five overarching themes of challenges related to the NAS-in-FL environment that are present in the NAS-in-FL literature: \textit{Client Resource Constraints}, \textit{Client Heterogeneity}, \textit{Architecture Search Coordination}, and \textit{Security}. We organised these themes into ... sub-themes. For each sub-theme we describe concrete challenges relating to that sub-theme as well as solutions proposed for it in the literature.

The severity and applicability of challenges depends on the FL system characteristics as well as the NAS archetype employed as a basis for NAS-in-FL. We qualify challenges and solutions accordingly.
