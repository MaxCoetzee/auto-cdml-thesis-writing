We identified x overarching themes of challenges where the dominant source of the challenge is a FL system characteristic: \textit{Client Resource Constraints}, \textit{Client Heterogeneity}, and \textit{Security}. For these themes the severity and applicability of the callenge varies slightly depending on the search strategy and search space used by the NAS-in-FL (performance evaluation is basically universally trainig for short rounds) method and the solutions are often specific to one kind of search strategy. Additionally, we grouped challenges where the challenge is closely tied to the architecture search strategy used by a NAS method into another theme \textit{Architecture Search Coordination}.
